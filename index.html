CUE 03 / AI TOOLBELT

Six AI tools. One studio.

Each of these would be its own browser tab on a normal stream setup — a moderation bot, a clip detector, a chat assistant, a title generator. In Cohost, they're all the same app, talking to each other.

01
AI Cohost

Talks to your chat. Out loud.

An on-screen orb with a voice. Reads donations and important messages aloud. Flags trolls. Replies to viewers when the chat is dead. Trained on stream culture, not corporate sentiment.

02
Auto Clipper

Catches the moment as it happens.

Listens for spikes in mic + chat energy. Saves the last 30 seconds the instant something pops off. Webm to disk, ready to post.

03
Highlight Reels

Best moments, stitched.

At end of stream, picks your top clips by audio energy and fuses them into a single mp4. Drop straight into Shorts/TikTok/Reels.

04
AI Mod

Flags trouble in real time.

Reads every message before it hits the overlay. Hides slurs, scam links, raid copypastas. Doesn't sleep, doesn't blink, doesn't get tired.

05
Voice Commands

"Switch to game scene." Done.

Hands on the keyboard, eyes on the screen, mouth says the rest. Scene switching, mic mute, clip-it, sidekick on/off — all by voice.

06
Title Writer

Names your clips and streams.

Reads what happened in the last 60 seconds and writes a title that'll actually get clicked. Not "Cool clip 47" — something a viewer would tap.
